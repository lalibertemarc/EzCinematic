# EzCinematic

## v1.0.0

- First release for WoW Forever.
- `/cine` (or a key binding under Key Bindings → EzCinematic) toggles cinematic mode for screenshots: hides the UI, nameplates, NPC names and other players' names (yours stays), and applies EzSoundMixer's Music focus preset if EzSoundMixer is installed.
- Pressing it again puts back exactly what you had before, including your volumes. If you leave in combat, it waits until combat ends.
- A /reload or disconnect during cinematic mode restores your settings on the next login.
